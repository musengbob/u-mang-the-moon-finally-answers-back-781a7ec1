One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever once asked how I'm doing.

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) ($2 in USDC).

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry text to `entry.txt` (in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set, otherwise `output/`).
